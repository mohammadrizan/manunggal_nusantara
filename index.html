🌿 Activity Overview & Goal

Inspiring Young Innovators: Bringing Coding to Sekolah Manunggal Nusantara As part of Koding Next Batam’s mission to make technology accessible to young learners, our academic team led a dynamic, hands-on coding workshop at Sekolah Manunggal Nusantara. Designed for primary students from Grades 1 to 6, this initiative introduced fundamental computer science principles through age-appropriate, highly engaging activities—bridging the gap between creative play and computational logic.

01

Age-Differentiated Learning Pathways

Structured tailored learning modules across all primary levels (Grades 1–6), ensuring concepts were accessible, progressive, and engaging for both lower and upper primary students.

02

Screen-Free "Unplugged" Computational Logic

Introduced core programming concepts—such as algorithms, sequencing, and pattern recognition—to younger students using tactile, screen-free "unplugged" activities that built foundational critical thinking before touching a computer.

03

Interactive Project-Based Coding with Scratch

Guided upper primary classes through hands-on block-based coding in Scratch, helping students build their first interactive animations and mini-games while applying loop constructs and conditional logic.

04

Gamified Collaborative Problem-Solving

Facilitated team-based logic challenges and interactive debugging games that encouraged students to troubleshoot problems together, fostering active participation and teamwork.

05

Sparking Early STEM Interest & Digital Literacy

Created an energetic, supportive environment that demystified technology, encouraging young minds to see computers not just as devices for consumption, but as creative tools for building the future.
